## Generation Prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Create one standalone logo concept for Glide, an urban daily transport app. Concept: Glide Wing, an abstract compact symbol consisting of exactly two forward-swept deep teal strokes, suggesting gliding movement and a lightweight wing. The two strokes have distinct lengths and taper smoothly toward the right with an elegant upward sweep, separated by generous clear negative space. Distinctive asymmetric silhouette, restrained youthful professional identity, readable at app-icon size. Flat solid deep teal #087f73 only. Transparent background, square canvas, centered symbol with even generous margin. No letters, no G, no circles, no dots, no map pins, no arrowhead, no Google-like forms or multicolour palette. No text or wordmark, no shadows, no gradients, no mockup, no container. Crisp clean vector-like edges. Deliver one mark, not a sheet of alternatives.
